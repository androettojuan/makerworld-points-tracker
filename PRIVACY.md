# Privacy policy — Points Tracker for MakerWorld

*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

Points Tracker for MakerWorld is an unofficial browser extension. It is not affiliated with MakerWorld or Bambu Lab.

## What the extension reads

When you click **Sync** (or every 3 hours while a makerworld.com tab is open), the extension asks MakerWorld for your points history and current points balance. It does this from your own open makerworld.com tab, using the session you are already signed in with.

## What it stores and where

The points history, the balance and the extension's settings are saved **only in your browser** (`chrome.storage.local`). The extension has no server. It does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else, and it does not use analytics, ads or trackers.

The extension never reads or stores your MakerWorld password.

## Removing your data

Open the extension → *Diagnostics* → **Delete all data**, or uninstall the extension. Either one deletes everything it saved.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/androettojuan/makerworld-points-tracker/issues.

# Política de privacidad — Points Tracker para MakerWorld

Points Tracker para MakerWorld es una extensión no oficial. No está afiliada a MakerWorld ni a Bambu Lab.

Al tocar **Sincronizar** (o cada 3 horas mientras haya una pestaña de makerworld.com abierta), la extensión le pide a MakerWorld tu historial y tu saldo de puntos. Lo hace desde tu propia pestaña de makerworld.com, con la sesión que ya tenés iniciada.

Esos datos se guardan **solo en tu navegador** (`chrome.storage.local`). La extensión no tiene servidor. No envía tus datos al desarrollador ni a nadie más, y no usa analíticas, publicidad ni rastreadores. Nunca lee ni guarda tu contraseña de MakerWorld.

Para borrar todo, abrí la extensión → *Diagnóstico* → **Borrar todos los datos**, o desinstalala.
